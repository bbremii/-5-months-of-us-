Our Story 💌

It’s kinda crazy thinking about how all of this started.

We went on that class trip to Canada together, and at the time, I barely knew anyone. I’m naturally pretty shy, so being around a whole group of people I didn’t really know was honestly a little scary.

But then there was you.

You basically took me under your wing and made sure I wasn’t just by myself the whole time. You made me feel comfortable, included, and like I actually had someone there with me. I don’t think either of us knew how much that little thing would end up meaning.

As the trip went on, we just kept getting closer. The conversations, the laughs, the little moments, and just being around each other made everything feel so natural.

And somehow, what started as a class trip to Canada turned into us.

To my baby, my handsome boy ❤️

I don’t even know where to start because there are so many things about you that I love, and I don’t think I’ve ever really put all of them into words.

There are little things about you that get me every single time.

Like your brown eyes. It’s actually hard to stare into them when you’re looking right back at me because for some reason, I suddenly feel like a little girl with the biggest crush all over again. 😭❤️ It’s like everything else disappears for a second and I’m just thinking, yeah… I really like this boy.

And your hair. I love running my hand through your black hair, especially when it’s all curly and wavy. I don’t know what it is about it, but I could honestly just sit there playing with it forever.

And then there’s your beard… or should I say your soon-to-be goatee? 😭

I love playing with it even though it’s literally a couple little hairs right now.

oop—who said that? 🤭

But seriously, it’s all those little details about you that I notice. Your smile, your expressions, the way you laugh, the way you look at me, and all the little things you probably don’t even realize I pay attention to.

I also love the way you care for me.

Even when you’re tired after work, you still make time for me. You could easily just want to rest and do your own thing, but you still find a way to talk to me, check on me, or spend some time with me. I notice that. And I appreciate it more than I probably say.

I love your personality too. The goofy side of you, the serious side, the quiet side, the annoying side—yes, that one too. 😭 And I love that I get to know all those different versions of you.

I don’t just love one thing about you. I love all the little pieces that make you you.

And sometimes I think about how none of this was supposed to happen.

We were just two people on a class trip to Canada. I didn’t really know anyone, I was shy, and somehow you decided to take me under your wing.

You probably didn’t know it at the time, but that little thing changed so much for me.

And now here we are.

Five months later. ❤️

Five months of conversations, laughs, random moments, learning each other, and becoming us.

And we still have so much ahead of us.

I want the matching Christmas pajamas. 😂
I want us to make terrible gingerbread houses.
I want to bake cookies with you and probably eat half the dough.
I want random late-night food runs.
I want stupid challenges and little adventures.
I want our beach picnic.
I want to go to Foster’s with you for absolutely no reason and eventually have those ridiculous shopping-cart races I’ve already imagined. 😭

I want the ordinary days just as much as the big ones.

Because I think those are going to be some of my favorite memories.

So, happy 5 months, my baby.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for choosing me, for making time for me, and for all the little things you probably don’t even realize I notice.

I hope you know how loved you are.

And I hope when you look back at this one day, you remember that there was a girl who noticed all those little things about you and loved every single one.

Here’s to our first five months…
and to all the chapters we haven’t written yet. ❤️

I love youuu, my handsome boy.

— your girl ♡
